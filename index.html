Amogha Scholars Program

Enabling Potential. Empowering Futures.

The Amogha Scholars Program aims to make quality education at IIT Kanpur more accessible to meritorious students from families with limited financial means. Established with the support of Amogha Welfare Foundation, the scholarship seeks to ease financial constraints, enabling students to focus on their education and make the most of the opportunities before them.

A Commitment to Educational Equity

The Amogha Scholars Program reflects the philanthropic vision of Mr Alok Vinod Agarwal, who completed his B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from IIT Kanpur in 1979 and IIM Ahmedabad, and the founder of Amogha Welfare Foundation.

His commitment to IIT Kanpur extends beyond his professional association with the Institute. He established the Class of 1979 Young Faculty Fellowship, supporting faculty across engineering and science departments, and has instituted multiple scholarships to support undergraduate students. Through these initiatives, he continues to contribute to academic excellence and expand access to educational opportunities.

Scholarship Eligibility

The Amogha Scholars Program is open to eligible first-year undergraduate students at IIT Kanpur who meet the following criteria:

Academic Merit

Students with an All-India Rank (AIR) of 2,000 or better in the Open Category (Common Rank List) may apply.

Family Income

The Total Family Annual Income (TFAI) must be below ₹12 lakh.

Fee Waiver

Applicants must not be receiving a fee waiver from the Institute.

Other Scholarships

Applicants must not be recipients of another donor-supported scholarship.

Selection Criteria

Among eligible applicants, students with a comparatively higher AIR (better rank) will be given preference for scholarship selection.

Scholarship Benefits

The scholarship supports students in pursuing their academic goals while recognising the importance of continued academic performance.

Scholarship Support

A scholarship of ₹2 lakh, covering 100% of the tuition fee, easing financial constraints so that students can focus on their education and pursue their academic goals.

Academic Performance

Recipients who achieve a Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) of 8.5 or above at the end of the academic year.

Additional Reward

May be considered for reimbursement towards living expenses equivalent to 40% of the annual tuition fee.

This additional benefit is subject to the donor’s discretion and the applicable terms of the scholarship.

Through the Amogha Scholars Program, a commitment to education becomes an opportunity for deserving students to realise their potential and shape their futures.
